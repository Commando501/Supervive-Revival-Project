0. WHY THIS EXPERIMENT EXISTS -- AND WHY IT IS THE OPPOSITE OF ARM H
=================================================================================================
ARM H chose 2^-10 = 0.0009765625 uu/s precisely so it could NOT perturb the system under test, and
it answered its question: ULokiCMC::StartNewPhysics 0x055C2430 RUNS [M].

Then the lane-2 adversarial verifier established a mechanism that undercuts ARM H's SECOND, weaker
observation ("something writes Velocity"):

  *** A SMALL NON-ZERO VELOCITY CAN CONVERT A NO-WRITE INTO A WRITE. ***
  On the below-tolerance arm of a GetSafeNormal-shaped block the three ucomisd at
  0x055B8838 / 0x055B883E / 0x055B884A all fall through to je 0x55B8865 and the write is SKIPPED.
  And in engine PhysFalling, 2^-10 gives SizeSq 9.5367431640625e-07 (0x035ED9B3 call 0x035F4620),
  after which 0x035ED9BB movups [rsi],xmm0 + 0x035ED9C3 movsd [rsi+0x10],xmm1 write Velocity on
  the <= 1e-3 arm.

Both ARM H flights put the sentinel there themselves, so every observation of Velocity CHANGING was
made in a world we perturbed. What is established is only:
  [M]             something writes the BOT Velocity once it holds a SMALL non-zero value
  NOT ESTABLISHED that anything writes Velocity when it is EXACTLY ZERO

*** IF EXACTLY-ZERO SKIPS THE WRITE PATH, THE STANDING NULL IS A FIXED POINT: zero -> no write ->
stays zero. That is a MUCH SIMPLER WALL than a routine that computes zero, and it predicts that a
LARGE injected Velocity would PERSIST and MOVE THE PAWN. ***

ARM J tests that prediction. It PERTURBS BY DESIGN. That is the point, and it is why 600 uu/s is
correct here and would have been wrong in ARM H.

=================================================================================================

 A. Velocity PERSISTS at ~600  AND  the pawn TRANSLATES (moved >> 0 uu)

       outcome and it reframes the remaining work entirely. *****                            [M]

 B. Velocity PERSISTS at ~600  AND  the pawn does NOT translate
    -> the mover does not consume Velocity. The wall is BETWEEN Velocity and the actual move
       (UpdateComponentVelocity / MoveUpdatedComponent / the safe-move chain), NOT in
       CalcVelocity at all.                                                                  [M]

 C. Velocity DECAYS toward 0 across samples (a profile, not a step)
    -> something DAMPS it. The decay profile is itself diagnostic: a smooth decay implicates
       braking/friction (ARM G wrote GroundFriction 8 and BrakingDecelerationWalking 2048), a
       step to 0 implicates a hard clamp. REPORT THE PROFILE, do not summarise it.           [M]

    -> a hard zeroing site fires on a large Velocity too. That REFUTES the fixed-point
       hypothesis and puts CalcVelocity's clamp (or a sibling) back in play.                 [M]

    -> a real GAS-treatment dependency in the MOVER, not just in Acceleration. Report it as the
