## 3. THE INSTRUMENT'S BLIND SPOTS — stated, and quantified where possible

**(a) RPC send-stub substitution — the one the handoff named.** For a `Net` key the record's `+0x18` is the **UHT-generated send stub**, not the `_Implementation`.
**[M] 360 records carry `FUNC_Net`; the attribution-independent union with the `Server|Multicast|Client` name prefix is 413 records = 2.54 % of the table.** Their verdicts: REAL 329 · FORWARDER 40 · DARK 36 · EMPTY 8 — and **all 8 EMPTY carry no `Net` flag** (`ServerDrawDebug*` ×5, `ServerSetHeroClass`, `ServerSetAbilityToLevel`, `APlayerController::ServerExec`), so **exactly 0 of 360 true RPCs read EMPTY.** That is the signature of the bias, not evidence of absence. **Never grade a `Net` key from this table.** ⚠ The number is a **floor**: 557 records have no unique UHT owner, so no flags.

**(b) Virtual-dispatch forwarder.** **1,153 records (7.08 %)** whose impl is `mov rax,[rcx]; jmp qword [rax+disp]` — the real body is `vtable[disp/8]` and the table cannot reach it. These **can** be empty: I reproduced lane 4's control on `ULokiPlayerDropPlaneComponent`'s vtable `0x8a22520` — `+0x4c0` REAL, **`+0x4c8` `0xf7ec20`**, `+0x4d0` REAL, `+0x4d8` REAL, **`+0x4e0` `0xf7ec20`**, `+0x4e8` REAL: **2 of 6 slots EMPTY**. Displacements are decodable from the bytes (e.g. `ALokiRoundGameMode::OnNewPhase` `vt+0xb08`, `CompleteRound` `vt+0xb10`, `RestartRound` `vt+0xaf8`), but no generic vtable→class map exists offline: **[M] MSVC RTTI is absent for every Loki class** (`.?AVALokiRoundGameMode@@`, `.?AVAActor@@` etc. all ABSENT; control: 632 `.?AV` occurrences exist for third-party libs).

**(c) `.text` coverage.** **3,092 records (19.00 %) — COVERAGE-BLOCKED, not ABSENT.** The impl *address* is known for all of them and is not any of the 5 folds. **2,935 of them sit at multiplicity-1 impl addresses**, which an ICF fold cannot be by definition; only **157 records** sit at multiplicity-≥2 impls, so that is the **upper bound on EMPTY hiding in the dark**. ⇒ true EMPTY is in **[515, 672]**, i.e. **3.16 %–4.13 %** of the table.

**(d) The Angelscript layer is invisible to this instrument entirely.** `ALokiDropShip`, `ALokiDropPod`, `ULokiRespawnComponent`, `ULokiActorPoolManager` have **0 records**; `SpawnDropPodForTeam`, `InitializeDropPod`, `QueueCrewForPodSpawn`, `Respawn`, `PrimePools` are absent **by name** (attribution-independent). Per FK-1 they are StaticJIT-compiled and REAL. **Not covered either way — this census says nothing about the AS half of the drop path.**

### VERDICT: **bounded for the drop, unbounded for the game — and the drop's bound is smaller than it looks**

**Bounded, for deploy specifically.** The blocker set is **enumerated and small (~23 native functions)**, and it is now written down per-record. Better: **almost every one is a pure state mutation on a replicated property, not a computation** — `AddPlayerToPlane`, `RemovePlayerFromPlane`, `AuthAddPlayer`, `AuthRemovePlayer`, `SetCanJump`/`AuthSetCanJump`, `SetDropLeader`, `AuthSetCurrentRideable`, `SetPlayerTeam`, `SetNumTeams`, `OverridePlaneLocations`. This project's safest write class (aligned data poke on a reflected UPROPERTY: hazard **0/22**) substitutes for a setter that returns void.
★ **A worked example, measured today:** `ULokiRideableComponent::AuthAddPlayer` is `ret 0`, but its two REAL readers agree on where the state lives — `ContainsPlayer` (`0x55d0270`) and `HasEverContainedPlayer` (`0x55dcaa0`) both open `mov rax,[rcx+0x120]; movsxd rcx,[rcx+0x128]` ⇒ **`TArray<UObject*> Players` at `+0x120` (Data) / `+0x128` (Num)**, with `PlayersInsideCount` at `+0x11c` (from `OnRep_PlayersInsideCount` `0x55e0fc0`) and `bCanExit` at `+0x118` (from `CanExit` `0x525c240`). **`AuthAddPlayer`'s entire observable effect is reproducible as a data write.** Two independent functions, same offsets — that is the control.
The two that are **not** pokeable are `ALokiGameMode::SpawnPlayer` (returns a pawn; FK-1 already closed `AvatarActor = NULL` on it) and `AuthBeginGlideDiveFromDropPod` (starts a movement mode).

**Unbounded, for gameplay.** The same cut takes `ALokiCharacter::Gather*Movement` (all 6 — the networked movement gather), every `ALokiPlayerState::Add*Stat` (11) and 13 of its stat getters, `ALokiBaseItem` (23), `ALokiProjectile` (8), `ALokiMinionCharacter` (9), `ALokiTower` (7), `ULokiAbilitySystemComponent` authority (5), missions (5), XP. **And the honest counterweight to my own bound:** this census covers only the **native** half. The Angelscript half — which is where `SpawnDropPodForTeam` lives, and which S130/S131 just proved works — is **completely outside the instrument**. Whether AS supplies an alternate route around each of the 23 stubs is the question this lane cannot answer, and it is the thing that decides whether 23 is the real number or an over-count.
