## Known limits (read before trusting a line of this)

- **Locals have no names.** `v12` is a stack offset, not a source name.
  Their TYPES are real: object locals come from the function record's
  `ObjVariableTypes`, primitives are inferred from the signature of the
  call they are passed to.  `auto` means neither source knew.
- **No line numbers.** Statement order is bytecode order, not source order.
- **12 function(s)** could not be folded into `if`/`while` and appear in flat
  label+`goto` form.  Nothing is lost; it is just less pretty.
- **5 function(s)** leave the symbolic value stack non-empty at a `return`.
  Both an independent dword-depth counter (driven by the game's own
  `stackInc` table) and the symbolic stack agree on which ones, so these
  are the cases where the argument model is genuinely incomplete --
  treat their pseudo-source with suspicion and read the disassembly.
- Computed jumps (`JMPP`, 5 sites) are NOT reconstructed; they are marked
  `// computed jump -- flow not reconstructed`.
- The disassembly under every function is the ground truth and is
  independent of the pseudo-source: it is a direct decode of the bytes
  with symbol names attached, and it round-trips to the exact declared
  length for all 1463 functions.
